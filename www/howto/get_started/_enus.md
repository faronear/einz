# Einz - How to get started

Four steps from download to your first message.

## Step 1: Download and install

Get the build from [GitHub releases](https://github.com/faronear/einz/releases/tag/latest):

- **Android**: download the APK and allow installing apps from unknown sources;
- **iPhone**: download and trust the developer profile when prompted (not on the App Store);
- **Mac**: download and unzip the archive, then drag Einz into Applications.

## Step 2: Create a space

Follow the wizard on first launch:

1. Enter **your name** and gender (your companion will see them);
2. Pick a type: **duo space** (for two) or **group space** (for a small circle you choose);
3. Set the **shared passphrase** — the "key" to the space; your companion needs it to join, so remember it;
4. Set a device **PIN** — required every time you open the app, for the moment your phone isn't in your hands.

## Step 3: Invite your companion

Open the menu and tap **Invite** to generate an **entrance code**, then send it through whatever channel you already share (in person, SMS, chat). The code is valid once, within 24 hours.

Your companion installs the app, chooses **Join a space** at launch, enters the code and the shared passphrase — and they're in.

> There are three kinds of codes: **Invite** (bring in someone new), **Add a new entrance of mine** (for your own new device), and **Re-invite** (when your companion has lost all their entrances).

## Step 4: Start chatting

- Send text, photos, videos, voice notes, and files — like any chat app;
- Long-press a message → **Burn after reading** to set when it disappears;
- One check = sent, two checks = received;
- Switch between multiple spaces from the top bar; find **My entrances** and the shared passphrase in the menu.
