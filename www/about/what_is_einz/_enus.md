# Einz - What is Einz?

---

Einz is an end-to-end encrypted app for private messaging and sharing. It is not built for mass social networking — it belongs to two people, or a small circle of people you choose.

## No third party can read it

Not even the one running the server.

- Messages, photos, videos, voice notes, and files are encrypted **on your device**; the server only ever sees ciphertext;
- Keys never enter the server's database — they live only on your device and your companion's;
- If the server goes down, gets dumped, or gets audited, all anyone finds is noise.

## Works offline

Local-first: messages are stored on your device first and synced when you're back online. On a plane, on the subway, in the mountains — keep chatting; everything catches up automatically.

## What you get

- **Duo spaces**: a space for the two of you;
- **Group spaces**: a small circle of people you choose, in the same end-to-end encrypted space;
- **Rich messages**: text, photos, videos, voice notes, and files;
- **Burn after reading**: long-press any message to set when it disappears;
- **Read receipts**: one check = sent, two checks = received;
- **Multiple spaces**: one phone can hold several spaces, fully separated;
- **Your entrances**: one entrance per device — revoke a lost device anytime; a shared passphrase and PIN guard the door.

## What you don't get

No ads, no subscriptions, no feeds, no "people you may know". Not on any app store — download the build straight from [GitHub releases](https://github.com/faronear/einz/releases/tag/latest).

## Who it's for

For two people who take "private" seriously: partners, best friends, family. Not for crowds chasing noise.
