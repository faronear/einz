### 2026-10-03 Group spaces

Einz is no longer for two people only. You can now create a **group space** and bring a small circle of people you choose into the same end-to-end encrypted space; group read receipts show a headcount — the number of people who have received a message appears next to the double check.

### 2026-09-25 My entrances

The menu now includes **My entrances**: every entrance of yours on this and your other devices, with one-tap revocation for a lost device. The current entrance can be renamed.

### 2026-08-30 Interface language

A full English interface is here: Settings → Interface language, switch between 中文 and English anytime.
